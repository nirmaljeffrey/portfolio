GDG Android Munich Meetup in April 2026

An Android community meetup where I played a key role in helping AutoScout24 host the local Android community.

April 2026 | AutoScout24 Munichmap[email:jeffattry@gmail.com name:Nirmal Jeffrey]

I played a key role in helping my employer, AutoScout24, host this GDG Android Munich meetup for Android developers, Kotlin engineers, and people exploring mobile development.

AutoScout24 Office Meetup

  • Helped get approval for the local GDG community to host the event at AutoScout24.
  • Helped plan the format, coordinate the venue, and host the evening.
  • Event page: Apres-ski Meetup on Meetup
  • Venue: AutoScout24 GmbH, Munich.
  • Talks covered trunk-based Android development, Android code creation with Figma MCP and Claude, and Kotlin Multiplatform from an Android engineering perspective.

Speakers:

  • Vadim Schenev: Shipping Android Fast: One Codebase, Many Markets.
  • Hans-Martin Schmargendorf: From Design to Code: Using MCP, Claude, and Documentation to Accelerate Android Development.
  • Daniel M. Idrees: One Codebase, Many Platforms: Kotlin Multiplatform for Android Engineers.
