A short note on what's currently on my bench, mostly so future-me remembers what this month felt like.

## Pose Studio

The big one is [Pose Studio](/projects/pose-studio-waitlist), a camera app for iOS and Android that gives real-time pose guidance from a quick draft photo. It's the first app I'm shipping under Vaazh Studios, and there's a waitlist open while I get it ready.

## On the workshop side

The 3D printer has barely cooled down lately. Most of it is functional prints — brackets, jigs, repairs — but a few playful builds have snuck in too. I'll write those up properly once they actually work.

## What's next

I want to get into a rhythm of shipping small things often, and writing a little about each one. If that sounds like your kind of thing, the [projects page](/projects) is the best place to follow along.
