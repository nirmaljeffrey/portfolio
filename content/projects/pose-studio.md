**[Pose Studio](https://posestudioapp.com/)** is an AI camera app I built for iOS and Android — coach your pose, own the pic. Take a quick draft photo and it gives real-time pose guidance so the next shot looks natural instead of stiff.

- Site: [Pose Studio — posestudioapp.com](https://posestudioapp.com/)
- Status: **Live** on iOS and Android
- Platforms: iOS and Android

Built with **Kotlin Multiplatform** and **Compose Multiplatform** — one shared codebase across both platforms — with a Firebase + AI backend. It's the app the Snavo codebase later grew out of.
